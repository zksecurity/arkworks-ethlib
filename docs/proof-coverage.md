The core contracts require stored Montgomery residues below the modulus; public
constructors and exporters accept/return canonical integers. G1 subgroup classification
assumes an on-curve input unless the function explicitly performs that validation.
The decoder APIs validate their byte inputs rather than assuming canonical coordinates.
Decoder proofs establish matching returned points and acceptance/rejection, with EthLib
error variants erased to `Option`. `uncompress_g1` accepts full-curve points;
`decode_g1` also checks the subgroup, and `decode_public_key` also rejects infinity.

The proof files are [field arithmetic](../curve/FieldRefinement.lean),
[Montgomery multiplication](../curve/MontgomeryProof.lean), [Montgomery conversion](../curve/MontgomeryConversionProof.lean), [point recovery](../curve/RecoveryProof.lean), [G1 operations](../curve/ConcreteProof.lean), [encoding/validation](../curve/EncodingProof.lean),
[point helpers](../curve/PointProof.lean), [extension arithmetic](../curve/ExtensionProof.lean),
[Miller computation](../curve/PreparedMillerProof.lean), [pairing equivalence](../curve/PreparedPairingEquivalence.lean),
and [decoding](../curve/DecoderProof.lean).
Published-vector Lean checks are generated by `make verify-vectors`.

These are implementation-refinement results. Mathematical pairing laws, protocol
security, universal equivalence to the full arkworks crate, native compilation, and
full precompile gas/error behavior remain outside the proofs.
